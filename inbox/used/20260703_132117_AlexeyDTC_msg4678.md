Hi All,

Would it be possible to organize book-reading sprints alongside the technical sessions?
There are several books and long-form resources that many of us want to read but struggle to finish consistently, for example:
 LLM Handbook
 AI Engineering by Chip Huyen
 Hamel Husain's work on evaluations (Evals)
 Other high-quality papers, blogs, and engineering handbooks
A sprint could work similarly to coding sprints:
 Read a fixed number of chapters/pages each week.
 Hold a weekly discussion or Q&A.
 Share key takeaways, notes, and implementation ideas.
 End with a small project or presentation applying what we learned.
I think this would help members build deeper understanding, stay accountable, and learn from one another rather than reading everything alone. It could become a great complement to the hands-on engineering curriculum.
