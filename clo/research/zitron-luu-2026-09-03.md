# Dan Luu audits Ed Zitron

## The essay (danluu.com/zitron/, ~Sep 1-3 2026)
- Luu's disclosure: no financial dog in the fight (index funds only, failed an AI lab phone screen years ago); 2022 futurist-predictions audit (Kurzweil etc. wrong on results AND reasoning); 2015 piece arguing people underestimate AI job displacement. Position: "if something is currently happening, people saying it's impossible are probably wrong."
- Method: audited dated, falsifiable Zitron predictions Feb 2024 - Oct 2025 (~25 items)
- Result: essentially ALL wrong, on both outcomes and reasoning

## Mechanics of the genre (Luu's analysis)
- Authority laundering: cited as "the guy who looked at the numbers"
- Anger as engagement driver
- Gish gallop as "amplification DoS": 90 seconds of talk ≈ 5k words to refute; cheap to generate, expensive to correct
- Spreadsheet errors: Timothy B. Lee found Feb 30, March counted twice, Aug 21-Oct 21 as one month in Zitron's Anthropic projection ($3.6B claim resolves once fixed) https://x.com/binarybits/status/2034377838883700953
- Juho Snellman (HN): "economic analysis is absolute trash... pretty clearly intentional deception. Every article is a 10k word gish gallop" https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45402639
- Extreme stated confidence on every call (Luu: never been wrong at that confidence level; single miss = overconfidence, routine misses = pattern)
- Contradiction catch (Dennis Snell): Google "doesn't know how to grow" + "500M users absurd" can't both be true (they could stick Gemini everywhere)
