## 4. Project evidence: what was publicly showcased

The three instructor showcases contain 26 participant project entries: four in the first showcase, nine in the second, and 13 in the third. The first also includes one instructor reference project. One later project has two coauthors, so these are not unique-person, graduate or enrollment counts. Seventeen linked student-owned repository pages were opened; three READMEs received closer connected-GitHub inspection. The applications were not executed, security-audited or independently benchmarked. ### Third cohort: thirteen project entries

Salma Bouzid — Quizgen / Exam Questions Generator. Lecture PDFs to editable exam questions, with approve/edit/reject flow and grounding checks. Showcase and demo description; no repository linked. [[S04](https://aishippingblog.com/p/13-ai-projects-from-ai-engineering)]

Amar Agrawal — ATS Gap Analyser. CV/job-description comparison, suggested improvements and cover letters. README documents tests, labeled scenarios and limitations; judge metrics must be distinguished from user outcomes. [[P01](https://github.com/Amar-Ag/ats-gap-analyser)]

Leo Cabibihan — Chess Coach Agent. Imported games become weaknesses and practice sessions; Stockfish, tests, feedback and fallback logic. Public README contains evolving/partly inconsistent descriptions of where retrieval and the agent run. Avoid an overly specific architecture claim without a code audit. [[P02](https://github.com/leo-cabibihan/chess-coach-agent)]

Paulien Out and Alena Fojtik — Document Preparation Agent. PDF preparation and metadata manifests for retrieval. Public sample documents, not exposure of private client material. [[P03](https://github.com/PaulienOut/rag-dataprep-agent)]

Hana Ben Ali — Research Radar. Paper discovery, personalized ranking and feedback. The showcased ranking improvement is a small self-rated evaluation, not a general research-productivity result. [[P04](https://github.com/55382/Research_Radar_agent_phd_assistant)]

Thet Su Win — AI Diet Coach. Southeast Asian meal-planning prototype with a curated recipe collection and evaluation. Not a medical or dietary-outcome claim. [[P05](https://github.com/thetsuwin66/ai-diet-coach-agent)]

Wesley Tan — AI Learning OS. Separates queued material from knowledge actually studied; includes retrieval and an empty-knowledge-base check. The reported 15/15 applies to a small tuning set, not universal accuracy. [[P06](https://github.com/wesleytanjiale/ai-learning-os)]

Marco Teran — Applied ML Teaching Copilot. Answers grounded in course materials. A judge’s perfect score on 33 labeled cases is not evidence of perfect production answers. [[P07](https://github.com/marcoteran/applied-ml-teaching-copilot)]

Lars van Asseldonk — Datawarehouse Agent. Question refinement followed by read-only SQL and traceable results. Uses locally seeded data; Docker and CI are listed as future work. [[P08](https://github.com/larsvasseldonk/datawarehouse_agent)]

Miki Foster — Work Chronicle / WorkerChronicle. Career-evidence library for grounded application writing and interview preparation. Not evidence of increased hiring success. [[P09](https://github.com/MikiYamFos/work-chronicle)]

Nitesh Mishra — Multi-Agent Software Development Platform. Requirements flow through planning, human approval, architecture, code, QA and review stages. Presence of these stages does not independently prove generated-code quality. [[P10](https://github.com/mishranitesh/AI_Engineering_Buildcamp_From_RAG_to_Agents/tree/main/capstone/multi-agent-dev-platform)]

Dianne Bronola — LearnMate AI. Video-based concept extraction, questions, hints and practice. No independently measured learning-gain claim. [[P11](https://github.com/dinobronx/learnmate-ai)]

Katja Weber — GapFinder. Diagnoses understanding against video transcripts and recommends sections to revisit. Public artifact, not a controlled learning experiment. [[P12](https://github.com/katjaweb/gapfinder)]
