# Course Promotion Through Podcast Guests

I've come up with a plan for promoting my new course. I'll look online for people who have already talked about the subject. I'll invite them to the DataTalks.Club podcast to share their experience with our students. I can share our existing podcast and explain that we've been doing this for a long time.[^1]

I'll take registrations through Maven instead of Luma. That way, the waiting list for my Maven school will grow. I'll also get interesting content from the guests that I can draw on in the course.[^1]
