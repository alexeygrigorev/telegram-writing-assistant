# AI Shipping Labs Website Feedback (2026-07-13)

Feedback on the current state of the AI Shipping Labs website, recorded as voice notes. The speaker refers to Alexey in the third person, so this is feedback from a team member reviewing the site.

## Main page vs Community Overview page

I wanted to record feedback about the website. I am looking at the main page of AI Shipping Labs, and there is also an Overview section under Community, which I am looking at too[^1].

It is not clear to me by what logic the first page was separated from the second. Why did an overview page for the community appear, and by what principle was the information chosen for it versus the main landing page? Our main offer, AI Shipping Labs, is the community itself. So in my view, the information that now lives in the Community section should be on the main page. It should all be combined in one place, so that a person who comes to the site understands what exactly they get: that AI Shipping Labs is a community, what we do here, what activities we have, what the advantage of joining is, and only then the pricing[^1].

## The Plan, Sprint, Ship section and sprint landings

About the "before you commit" part: first about Plan, Sprint, Ship. As a person who does not know what this community is, I scroll down to this section, and it offers me the current or next sprint. I click, for example, "view this sprint", and a page opens that only says "login to join" plus a list of sprint calls. There is no information about the sprint itself[^1].

This is exactly what we talked about at the meeting and recorded as an improvement idea: make a landing page for the sprint itself. For example, each sprint gets its own landing page, or there is one shared landing page for all sprints, so a person understands what a sprint includes. I think a separate landing per sprint is better, because the sprints differ from each other[^1].

## The "Sample the community before you commit" section

About the "sample the community before you commit" part: in my view, the choice of sections shown on the main page is not clear in general - why exactly these sections were added there. This section in particular does not carry much meaning, because we do not have that many events a person can join for free. Almost all events are made for people who are already inside the community on the main plan[^2].

Right now there is one upcoming event, mock interviews for AI engineering roles, and event recordings that are available for free. And those are not really connected to the community, because they are mostly events Alexey recorded for DataTalks.Club - just free workshops - and he added them to the AI Shipping Labs workshop recordings. When a person looks at these recordings, the videos and the text notes, I do not know how much that gives them an idea of what the community is and what it looks like[^2].

## Show the activities that actually exist

On the AI Shipping Labs page I would like to focus on the activities we really already have, and display them. As we discussed on the call, after the community launch we had a landing page where I wrote out the main points of what is included in the community and what a person gets when they join. I would like to see that on the main page[^3].

## The Activities page in Community

I also went into Activities inside Community. The interface is not quite clear to me either. There are All, Basic, Main, Premium filters, but nothing changed here - it is the same as it was on the old version of the site. The idea was to shorten this list of activities and add only the ones we need[^3].

Then, for some reason, buttons appeared at the top: Compare pricing, Community sprints, Live events, Workshops. I thought clicking a button would take you down the page to a specific place on the landing, but these buttons lead to other pages. In my view this breaks the user's path: I came to look at one thing, I get thrown somewhere else, and then to return I have to press back and then click Live events. Instead of putting everything in one place, the user has to click buttons and move to new pages[^3].

## The Workshops section

The Workshops section also had the task of getting a landing page. Here it got better, because a short description of what these workshops are appeared, and on the right there is a grid of three elements showing the main characteristics of these events. But it is still not quite a landing page[^4].

The idea, as I understood it initially, is that a landing page helps the user orient themselves in the content that exists on the site. Each piece of information has its own place on this one long landing page. What exists now - this overview above the list of workshops - does add understanding for the user, but there is too much information in one place[^4].

The third point is that we will not have the workshop cover images. That means the cards need to change. Right now the covers simply went away, but the card itself stayed the same, and there is just a graduation cap icon in place of the image. If there are no pictures, that icon should not be added at all[^4].

Then, when I look at View All Workshops, All, Free, and Paid buttons were added - that is convenient. But Browse by Topic has a long list of topics, and different content classifications are mixed together there: one is by workshop theme, another is by the technologies we use inside, and a third is by the goal and what the person will get as a result of completing the workshop. In my view this is not very convenient to use[^4].
