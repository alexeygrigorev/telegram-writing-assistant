# Start Small Stay Small - Book Notes

Notes from reading "Start Small Stay Small" by Rob Walling.

## Setting measurable goals

I do not set goals - things just flow on their own. But for AI Shipping Labs to work, there needs to be a measurable goal. For example: 200 people in the community, and not just sitting there passively - there should be real activity between members[^1].

## Three roles, not just one

It is not enough to be a good technician. You also need to be a manager and an entrepreneur. On the technical side everything is fine, but the other two are lagging behind[^2].

## Priority ordering for a product

The book sorts what matters by importance[^3]:

1. Market - who are your customers
2. Marketing - how to reach them
3. Aesthetic design - how the product looks and feels
4. Functionality - what the product does

Without the market and marketing, it does not matter what features or design you have. And when everything else is equal, good design beats bad design. Features are the least important thing on the list[^3].

## Tour pages for websites

Every website should have a tour page. Right now neither the DataTalks.Club site nor the AI Shipping Labs site has one. Need to add a tour[^4].
