# Playbooks and Agents for DataTalks.Club Events and Content

Improvement ideas from Valeriia about extending the course promotion playbooks to events and other recurring DataTalks.Club activities, and handing part of that work to agents.

## Extend the playbooks to events

Valeriia is working on playbooks for promoting courses, and in parallel wants to schedule social media posts for promoting AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp, as usual. That led to another idea: since we already have playbooks for courses, we could do something similar for events - a repository for events, and maybe for other things that happen regularly at DataTalks.Club[^1].

The trigger for the idea was looking at the posts Grace publishes when she creates a new event, workshop, or podcast. Grace uses templates Valeriia wrote a long time ago for Francis, back when he was still working here. At that time AI was not really good yet - it did not give good results - and Francis was not particularly interested in it either, so Valeriia does not even remember whether he ever published posts using her templates. The templates are very generic: "join us on this podcast", "boost your...", "register for this event"[^1].

## Hand part of it to agents

It would be great if we could hand part of these tasks to agents, so that Grace could use them. Valeriia is not sure what the best way to do this is[^1].

One option: the same way we have an Airtable base where we add events, we would have the same thing on GitHub. Then she could run her agent once a week - or on whatever cadence we agree on - to create the posts, review them, and hand them to Grace to publish[^1].

Another option: come up with a process where Grace can run the agents herself, and Valeriia checks the output afterwards. Either way, the goal is that the quality of the posts is better than it is now[^1].

This may be useful for something else as well. Alexey said he wants to move off Airtable onto a server - AWS, as Valeriia understood it. So this could work in tandem with the agents in the future[^2].

## Guest intake form for podcasts

A separate comment about one specific process. When a guest comes in, Grace asks them across several messages what time is convenient, and then asks them to send their data. This takes time[^3].

Instead, the guest could be given a form to fill in themselves. Maybe such a process existed before and did not work - Valeriia does not know. But it seems more convenient both for Grace and for the guest: the guest arrives and immediately fills in all the information in one place, instead of opening email several times to upload things like a photo and links that Grace asks for. Everything would already be in one place[^3].
