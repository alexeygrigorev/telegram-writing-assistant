# Voice Guide Template

Source: Lieberman Content Machine [^1]

This template shows the structure of a voice guide file. In a real setup, `/content-setup` generates this by analyzing your top 10 or more actual posts.

## Structure

```markdown
# Voice Guide - [Your Name]

## Executive summary
[One paragraph capturing how you write. Example: "Maya writes like
an engineer filing a very confident bug report against the industry.
Declarative, numbers-first, dry humor, zero hype."]

## Core DNA
1. [First principle of your writing, e.g., "Receipts over rhetoric.
   Every claim ships with a number or a config snippet."]
2. [Second principle]
3. [Third principle]

## The #1 rule
[Your single most important writing rule, e.g., "Write like you're
texting a friend" or "Write like a post-mortem: what happened,
what it cost, what we changed."]

## Top 10 posts (the evidence)
1. > "[Exact text of your top post]"
   - Platform, performance note. Why it worked: [specific reason]
2. > "[Exact text]"
   - Why it worked: [reason]
[... continue to 10]

## Hook formulas actually used
- [Formula name]: [description] (posts N, N)
- [Formula name]: [description] (posts N, N)

## Content structures
- [Structure name]: [description] (posts N, N)

## Language patterns
- Sentence rhythm: avg ~[N] words; fragments every [N] sentences
- Recurring phrases: "[phrase]", "[phrase]"
- Never uses: "[word]", "[word]", "[word]"
- Punctuation: [habits]

## Format-specific notes
### X
[Platform-specific habits]

### LinkedIn
[Platform-specific habits]
```

## Key Principles

The voice guide is built from evidence (real posts), not aspiration. It captures what you actually do, not what you think you do.

The hook formulas come from reverse-engineering your top posts, not from a template. If your best hooks use two-number collisions, the guide says that. If they use counterintuitive results with the conclusion withheld, it says that.

The "never uses" list matters as much as the "always uses" list. Knowing that you never use exclamation points, never say "excited to announce", and never use the rocket emoji is voice information.
