# Interview Experiment

A running log of the experiment of going through interviews at different companies. The plan was mentioned before: start interviewing at various companies and see what the process looks like.

## Deriv - first interview with the founder

The first company reached out to me themselves. They need someone senior. The company is called Deriv. They seem to be from Malaysia, and in Malaysia they work on payments[^1].

The first interview was with the founder and took 15 minutes. He asked me to tell him about myself. I was not prepared for this at all, so I said whatever came out. I need to prepare better for this next time[^1].

What I told him: I started as a software engineer, then became a data scientist and machine learning engineer, and eventually became a principal. My task there was to oversee the machine learning platform at the company, convince everyone that data science is effective, improve the path to production, and make it easier to deploy models. Now I automate my own business so I can work more[^1].

### What the company is looking for

There is a big focus on automation with AI, across everything. They have hundreds of employees and have already automated many of the simple things. Now they are focusing on the more complex ones[^1].

They have very few senior people. They need people with 15-20 years of experience who can work at the architecture level. A lot of what they do is the software engineering process itself, and they have problems building specifications correctly - so that agents do not go off the rails and build the wrong thing[^1].

He also said they have a lot of data, and they are trying to use it in the business itself. So they need help not only from the software development process side, but also with AI engineering - using AI in the right way[^1].

### Where the company is

They have a payment system. Most people are in Dubai and in Malaysia. There are people in Berlin, 4 people in London, and a small office in Paris as well[^1].

### Next step

The next step is to talk with the team in Berlin. The Berlin office is at Potsdamer Platz, probably a WeWork[^1].
