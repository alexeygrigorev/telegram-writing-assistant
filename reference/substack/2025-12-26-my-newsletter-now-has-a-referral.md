## How to participate

**1. Share Alexey On Data.** When you use the referral link below, or the “Share” button on any post, you’ll get credit for any new subscribers. Simply send the link in a text, email, or share it on social media with friends.

[Refer a friend](https://aishippingblog.com/leaderboard?&utm_source=post)

2. **Earn benefits.** When more friends use your referral link to subscribe, you’ll receive special benefits.

* **Get access to the Tutorial Library (3 referrals):** A curated collection of practical, code-first materials, including workshops, hands-on projects, and study resources focused on learning by doing and solving real engineering problems.
* **Early access to an Exclusive Mini-Course (10 referrals):** First access to a new mini-course on building an action-oriented AI agent that interacts with external systems via APIs, covering tool design, agent loops, logging, and testing. The course is currently in development.
* **30-Minute 1:1 Zoom Chat with Alexey (25 referrals):** A private 30-minute Zoom conversation to discuss career decisions, learning paths, technical challenges, or project feedback, with no fixed agenda.
