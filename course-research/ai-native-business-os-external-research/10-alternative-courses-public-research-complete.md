# Alternative AI operations courses and instructor public research

**Research date:** 2026-09-01  
**Scope:** Nine alternative courses and the public digital footprints of the people or organizations behind them. Alexey's course appears only as a comparison baseline.

This is a best-effort, high-signal public-material sweep rather than a claim to have captured every social post ever published. It prioritizes official course pages, public syllabi, free lessons, framework pages, demonstrations, long-form articles, podcasts, repositories and public posts that expose curriculum, operating assumptions or implementation mechanics. Email-gated resources are indexed but not reconstructed, and paid course content is not reproduced.

## Central conclusion

A large share of the **knowledge layer** of these courses can be reconstructed from public material:

- workflow discovery and current-state mapping;
- value, feasibility and risk framing;
- context, data and human/AI authority design;
- Skills, agents and no-code automation patterns;
- APIs, webhooks, connectors and data movement;
- evaluations, reviewers, approval gates and basic observability;
- governance, adoption, ownership and maintenance.

Public material does not reproduce the **implementation service layer**: confidential diagnosis, feedback on a real process, access and integration debugging, failure analysis on company data, stakeholder alignment, shipping accountability, controlled go-live and production stabilization. This is the layer that can justify a premium engagement.

## The three schools in the market

### Workflow intelligence, economics and human redesign

Nucleations, HBS, Lena Shakurova and Han Kay ask why the workflow should change, where value and burden reside, how authority and roles change, and whether the intervention is economically and organizationally viable.

### Personal context and AI orchestration

Rona Ruthen, Sash Mohapatra and Happy Operators focus on turning AI from a blank chat into a persistent operating environment with context, reusable capabilities, specialist agents, connectors and review routines.

### Integration and production mechanics

Le Wagon, Happy Operators, Lena Shakurova and parts of the Coursera specialization emphasize data movement, triggers, actions, tool integration, testing and monitoring.

# Course-by-course research

## 1. Rona Ruthen — AI-First Operators

Rona's public footprint is one of the richest in concrete demonstrations. Her recurring topics are operational resilience, persistent context, executive preparation, customer insight, agent teams, quality assurance and adoption.

### Recoverable framework

The public material suggests this operating pattern:

> Persistent business context → reusable capability or Skill → specialist agents → independent validator/challenger/persona review → human approval → execution → post-run learning.

The important idea is not merely using several agents. The agents receive distinct responsibilities, and generation is separated from checking. Learning from a completed run is fed back into the operating context rather than being left inside one chat.

Rona also treats AI delegation as a graduated trust relationship. The AI system needs competence, context, feedback and oversight; the human retains accountability. Her broader public writing focuses on what happens when managers oversee people, workflows and agents simultaneously.

### Best public materials

- [Build Resilience into Your Workflows with Claude Code](https://maven.com/p/0a68cf/build-resilience-into-your-workflows-with-claude-code) — persistent context, multi-agent board preparation, QA validation, strategic challenge, persona review and a post-meeting learning loop.
- [Build a Voice-of-the-Customer Dashboard with Claude and Lovable](https://maven.com/p/89a77d/build-with-claude-lovable-voice-of-the-customer-dashboard) — raw data analysis, root-cause themes, human PII/data-quality checks, KPI selection and dashboard publishing.
- [Build a Claude Code Operating System for Fractional Leaders](https://maven.com/p/75e364/build-a-claude-code-operating-system-for-fractional-leaders) — system, project and client context; memory; meeting preparation and follow-up; task management; QA.
- [AI for Operators program page](https://ronaru.com/program) — audience, operating-system framing and desired business outcomes.
- [Rona Ruthen Maven profile](https://maven.com/ronaruthen) — index to workshops and public lessons.
- [Be a Goldfish: persistent systems for fractional work](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rona-ruthen_be-a-goldfish-its-one-of-my-favourite-activity-7495370179141402624-pTRm) — externalized client context and routines rather than reliance on conversational memory.
- [Leadership in a hybrid human-AI operating world](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rona-ruthen_some-days-i-feel-like-im-flying-through-activity-7446560534616780800-ouYM) — managing humans, workflows, agents and self.
- [Resilience by Design](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rona-ruthen_last-week-at-the-brilliant-alma-angels-summit-activity-7475428842027814912-FUXT) — QA inside the pipeline and systems that continue without the builder.
- [AI delegation and trust](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rona-ruthen_in-recent-conversations-a-few-people-told-activity-7449084095118262272-2TsE) — skills, context, trust, feedback and oversight.
- [Operations for a Hyper-Growth Start-Up](https://feeds.buzzsprout.com/1205177.rss) — broader operations background useful for interpreting the AI examples.

### What can be learned free

Persistent context, multi-agent role separation, validator and challenger gates, customer-analysis workflows, meeting preparation, post-run learning and adoption-oriented operator design.

### What the paid program still adds

A coherent sequence of builds, direct feedback, shipping accountability, help integrating multiple artifacts into one operator system and support for team adoption.

### Relationship to Alexey

This overlaps primarily with **Specify, Build, Prove and Run**. Rona's public material is stronger on agent-team choreography, operator adoption and polished demonstrations. Alexey can differentiate with deeper process economics, production acceptance, controlled go-live and company-level ownership.

## 2. Matt Roberts and Rafa Romero — Happy Operators / Claude at Work

Happy Operators publishes unusually detailed role-specific syllabi for agencies, marketers and freelancers. The examples make the learning concrete: statements of work converted into structured data, mixed exports normalized into common tables, receipts converted into tax-ready records, scheduled lead triage, proposal-generation Skills, connector-based workflows and handover context for another operator.

### Recoverable framework

The public sequence is approximately:

> Create a quick first win → choose the right Claude mode → add connectors and context → refine the repeated procedure → package it as a Skill → schedule it → test messy inputs → create a handover.

Matt also publicly advocates a disciplined agent loop:

> Plan → Implement → Test → Review.

This is combined with grounding and independent verification: one model or workflow generates the work and another check verifies structure, facts or requirements.

Rafa's public Hapi material adds a product and privacy perspective. His reusable pattern is:

> Capture unstructured voice → transcribe → extract structured information → route it into the applications where action occurs.

The local-first orientation is a useful reminder that architecture should follow privacy and workflow constraints, not convenience alone.

### Best public materials

- [Claude at Work: Practical Automation for Non-Developers](https://maven.com/happy-operators/become-happy-operator) — Skill, Cowork automation, scraper, dashboard, messy-input testing and handover.
- [Claude Cowork for Agencies](https://maven.com/happy-operators/claude-cowork-for-agencies) — mode routing, SOW-to-CSV verification, connectors, proposal Skills, context engineering and lead triage.
- [Claude Cowork for Marketers](https://maven.com/happy-operators/claude-cowork-for-marketers) — data normalization, verification scripts, branded reports, scheduled alerts and context packaging.
- [Claude Cowork for Freelancers](https://maven.com/happy-operators/claude-cowork-for-freelancers) — receipts, invoicing, reminders, inquiry triage, time tracking and VA handover.
- [Create a Successful Lead Magnet Strategy with Relay and Claude](https://maven.com/p/4f705a/create-a-successful-lead-magnet-strategy-with-relay-claude) — content engine, human curation, micro-tool and model optimization.
- [Structured AI workflows: Plan → Implement → Test → Review](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattjroberts_you-have-to-be-careful-when-building-ai-automations-activity-7399341705394728962-KOH_) — structure, retrieval grounding and multi-model verification.
- [What is Hapi?](https://speakhapi.com/blog/what-is-hapi) — local-first product and privacy design.
- [Voice Memos Transcription Workflow](https://speakhapi.com/blog/voice-memos-transcription-workflow) — capture, transcription, extraction and application integration.
- [launch-new-project repository](https://github.com/rafanocode/launch-new-project) — reusable project scaffolding.

The public pages name an **R·C·T·F** prompt framework, but they do not provide an authoritative public definition of every letter. It should not be reconstructed from similarly named prompting frameworks elsewhere.

### What can be learned free

Practical workflow assembly, context layers, mode selection, connectors, Skills, schedules, structured-output verification, messy-input testing, privacy-aware architecture and handover.

### What the paid program still adds

Project critique, direct support, implementation cadence and review of the learner's actual SOP and systems.

### Relationship to Alexey

This overlaps primarily with **Specify, Build and Prove**. Happy Operators is especially strong at approachable, tangible construction. Alexey can differentiate with workflow economics, production acceptance, incident readiness and company-wide operating controls.

## 3. Le Wagon — AI & Data Automation

Le Wagon provides the strongest public outline for the technical substrate beneath business automation. Its course progresses through APIs and JSON, data acquisition and cleaning, routing and AI decision layers, agents and memory, then monitoring, error handling, costs, logs, documentation and deployment.

### Recoverable framework

> Trigger → receive or retrieve data → validate and normalize → enrich → apply deterministic rules and/or an AI decision → take an action → log the result → handle errors.

This framework is valuable because it shows that the model is usually only one component. Much of the hard work is moving, cleaning, constraining and observing data.

### Best public materials

- [AI & Data Automation course](https://info.lewagon.com/ai-data-automation-course) — detailed public syllabus covering APIs, data, AI decisions, agents, monitoring, costs and deployment.
- [Build an AI Agent with n8n & Slack](https://www.lewagon.com/events/build-an-ai-agent-with-n8n-slack-2bOY5X) — narrow agent architecture, prompts, tools and limitations.
- [Créer un agent IA marketing avec n8n & Slack](https://www.lewagon.com/events/creer-un-agent-ia-marketing-avec-n8n-slack-SKYIsz) — French-language presentation of a similar build.
- [Build a Trip Planner with No-Code](https://www.lewagon.com/events/build-a-trip-planner-with-no-code-QXEA48) — separation of interface, data store and automation layers.
- [Grow Your Leads with API Automations](https://www.lewagon.com/events/grow-your-leads-with-api-automations-J2JaEB) — APIs and webhooks involving Shopify, Stripe, Mailchimp and a sales CRM.
- [Create Your First Client Tracker: Intro to APIs & Webhooks](https://www.lewagon.com/events/create-your-first-client-tracker-intro-to-apis-webhooks-csQuas) — triggers, payloads and integrations through a mini CRM.
- [Le Wagon free events library](https://www.lewagon.com/events) — index of automation, AI, data and no-code workshops.

### What can be learned free

APIs, webhooks, event-driven automation, data transformation, no-code integration and narrow agent patterns.

### What the paid program still adds

Intensive practice, instructor troubleshooting, project scoping, feedback and a completed deployable automation.

### Relationship to Alexey

## 4. Vanessa Rementilla and Saad Nizari — Nucleations / AI Workflow Design Lab

Nucleations is the strongest alternative for understanding work before building. Its materials emphasize the gap between the documented process and the process that is actually performed, including shadow work, workarounds, hidden decisions and human burden.

### Recoverable framework

The public ARIA descriptions reveal at least four mapping dimensions:

> Actions, decisions, roles and burden.

The broader flow is:

> Understand actual work → expose hidden work and judgment → redesign the future state → decide where AI earns a role → quantify operational and human value → assess feasibility and risk → package an executive investment case.

This approach rejects copying fashionable competitor use cases. The correct use case should emerge from the organization's own operational evidence.

The website also names an **IMPACT Model** and the course uses a **Human Dividend** lens. The complete proprietary component structure is not public. The safe public interpretation is that the future workflow should be judged by operational value, changed human burden and the cost/risk of the future state.

### Best public materials

- [AI Workflow Design Lab](https://maven.com/vanessa-rementilla/ai-workflow-designlab) — ARIA, Human Dividend, readiness, current-state mapping, redesign, role change, governance, feasibility and business case.
- [The Missing Method: Map Your Work Before You Build with AI](https://maven.com/p/137650/the-missing-method-map-your-work-before-you-build-with-ai) — actions, decisions, roles, burden, baselines and ROI.
- [Nucleations website](https://nucleations.ai/) — ARIA and IMPACT positioning.
- [Nucleations LinkedIn page](https://ca.linkedin.com/company/nucleations) — hidden work, handoffs, ownership, judgment and adoption.
- [Vanessa Rementilla](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/vrementilla) — human-centered design and transformation footprint.
- [Saad Nizari](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/saad-nizari) — AI strategy and investment-case footprint.
- [AI success depends on the problem and human experience](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nucleations_ai-mistakescopyvf-activity-7442655971694415872-yeJE) — avoiding tool-first selection and adoption as an afterthought.
- [AI adoption stalls when business value is unclear](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nucleations_intro-to-nucleations-activity-7439751920019243008-1T2h) — starting from the largest business problem and measurable value.

### What can be learned free

Workflow interviews, actual-work mapping, hidden-work analysis, role redesign, human burden, AI-readiness logic and executive business-case construction.

### What the paid program still adds

The complete proprietary mapping instrument, guided analysis, feedback and decision-ready packaging.

### Relationship to Alexey

This overlaps strongly with **Map and Specify**. Nucleations is stronger publicly on human-centered discovery and business-case design. Alexey can combine these ideas with an actual deployment, production controls and stabilization.

## 5. Sash Mohapatra — RiftLab / AI Orchestration Certification

Sash has one of the broadest directly reusable free libraries. The worldview is that AI leverage comes from infrastructure that remembers, accepts delegated work, uses tools and compounds through reusable capabilities—not from repeatedly opening an empty chat.

### Recoverable frameworks

The **Orchestrator Method** is publicly expressed approximately as:

> Define the end state → plan with AI → deconstruct and delegate → remain in the loop → build reusable systems.

Other useful public structures include:

- **Think / Remember / Act / Use Tools** for separating system capabilities.
- A persistent context file for identity, voice, standards and rules.
- A delegation matrix that varies autonomy according to impact and stakes.
- Decision Councils with independent functional views, a challenger, evidence standards and a pre-mortem.
- Workflow-to-Skill packaging only after the manual procedure has been refined.
- Separate generation and audit; keep secrets outside prompts.

The course also uses an **AIM** communication framework. Public descriptions indicate Ask with purpose, Include details and Modify/refine, but the definitive teaching context remains course-specific.

## 6. Han Kay — Sysdom / Build Your Agentic Company

Han publishes the most extensive long-form conceptual body of work. The practical core is useful even when separated from more theoretical consciousness and alignment material.

### Recoverable frameworks

The course models a venture through four engines:

- product or service;
- customer;
- cash;
- skills.

It asks participants to distinguish observations, assumptions and unverified claims, then identify the current constraint and create a bounded intervention.

The **Venture Constitution** classifies actions as delegated, notification-only, approval-required or prohibited. The operating cycle is approximately:

> Diagnosis → authorization → execution and evidence review → learning and the next cycle.

Another reusable diagnostic is:

> Surface → Engine → Driver → Pattern → Leverage.

The Agentic Collapse material argues that autonomy becomes fragile when agent activity grows faster than shared context, decision rights, evidence, constraint awareness and recovery mechanisms.

### Best public materials

- [Build Your Agentic Company](https://maven.com/hankay/systems-intelligence) — four engines, evidence-backed venture state, constraint, bounded intervention, Venture Constitution and operating cycle.
- [Conscious Systems](https://sysdom.ai/articles/conscious-systems) — long-form book/playbook with system laws, archetypes, cases and templates.
- [Span of Control field guide](https://maven.com/hankay/o/31d703) — four engines, leverage, authorization and evidence review.
- [ConsciOS preprint](https://maven.com/hankay/o/62fdf7) — theoretical background; treat as theory rather than validated implementation evidence.
- [Read Apple in 20 Minutes](https://sysdom.ai/articles/read-apple-in-20-minutes) — Surface → Engine → Driver → Pattern → Leverage.
- [Agentic Collapse](https://sysdom.ai/articles/agentic-collapse) — shared state, rights, provenance, recovery and cost controls.
- [Engineering Inner Alignment](https://sysdom.ai/articles/engineering-inner-alignment) — control-theoretic proposal that is explicitly not empirically validated.
- [The Sysdom Thesis](https://sysdom.ai/articles/the-sysdom-thesis) — whole-system intelligence and the danger of local optimization.
- [Why Sysdom Exists](https://sysdom.ai/articles/why-sysdom-exists) and [Sysdom article library](https://sysdom.ai/articles) — problem framing and full index.

### What can be learned free

Whole-company system mapping, constraint analysis, evidence confidence, authority design, provenance and recovery-oriented governance.

### What the paid program still adds

Guided application to the participant's venture, Sysdom access, feedback and completion of one operating cycle.

### Relationship to Alexey

## 7. Karim Lakhani and Iavor Bojinov — HBS Online AI for Leaders

HBS provides the strongest strategic and organizational layer. The materials treat AI as an operating-model shift rather than merely a productivity tool.

### Recoverable frameworks

The recurring HBS sequence is:

> Strategic alignment and data readiness → task/process portfolio → early use-case prioritization → build/buy/hybrid decision → development and evaluation → adoption → continuing management and scaling.

A useful public selection lens combines **frequency, value and risk**. Frequently performed, measurable work can be attractive, but the cost of errors determines how much authority and human review are acceptable.

Bojinov's public initiative lifecycle is:

> Selection → development → evaluation → adoption → management.

Cost framing includes infrastructure, integration, maintenance and iteration, and human capital—not just the initial build.

# Unified free self-study curriculum

The following sequence reconstructs the most useful public knowledge without pretending it replaces confidential implementation work or production stabilization. Do not advance because the reading is finished; advance when the artifact passes the exit test.

## 1. Frame the business outcome

**Objective:** Define the operational problem, baseline, target and economic reason to act before selecting a tool.

- Primary: [What Does It Mean to Be AI-First?](https://online.hbs.edu/blog/post/ai-first)
- Secondary: [The Missing Method](https://maven.com/p/137650/the-missing-method-map-your-work-before-you-build-with-ai)
- Artifact: a one-page intervention thesis covering current pain, affected people, volume, baseline, desired outcome and non-goals.
- Exit test: a skeptical executive can understand why the workflow matters and which metric should move.

## 2. Map the work as it actually happens

**Objective:** Capture triggers, inputs, steps, decisions, handoffs, exceptions, workarounds, hidden judgment and human burden.

- Primary: [The Missing Method](https://maven.com/p/137650/the-missing-method-map-your-work-before-you-build-with-ai)
- Secondary: [Modern Chatbot Development Team Guide](https://shakurova.io/blog/what-each-modern-chatbot-development-team-needs-to-know)
- Artifact: interview two people who do the work; create a current-state map and mark undocumented steps, rework and judgment.
- Exit test: a frontline operator recognizes the map and can identify exceptions without rewriting it.

## 3. Select the right first workflow

**Objective:** Score candidates by recurrence, value, measurability, data/access, risk, reversibility, owner readiness and effort.

- Primary: [AI Delegation Decision Matrix](https://maven.com/sashmohapatra/o/fe9ac7)
- Secondary: [How to Decide Which Tasks to Delegate](https://hbr.org/2017/07/how-to-decide-which-tasks-to-delegate)
- Artifact: list five candidates; apply 6T, impact/stakes and ROI-feasibility scoring; document why one is selected and the others wait.
- Exit test: the chosen workflow has a clear owner, accessible inputs, enough test volume and value materially above operating cost.

## 4. Specify the end state and authority

**Objective:** Define the output, definition of done, AI/human responsibilities, approval points, prohibited actions and escalation boundaries.

- Primary: [Span of Control field guide](https://maven.com/hankay/o/31d703)
- Secondary: [Automate Your Workflows with Claude Skills](https://maven.com/p/6668be/automate-your-workflows-with-claude-skills)
- Artifact: an authority matrix covering delegated, notify, approval-required and prohibited actions, with one owner per decision.
- Exit test: no step has ambiguous accountability and every high-impact action has a named control.

## 5. Build the context architecture

**Objective:** Externalize stable business knowledge, process rules, examples, standards, data sources and project state.

- Primary: [Your CLAUDE.md](https://maven.com/p/511d6e/your-claude-md-the-file-that-makes-claude-code-yours)
- Secondary: [Claude Code OS for Fractional Leaders](https://maven.com/p/75e364/build-a-claude-code-operating-system-for-fractional-leaders)
- Artifact: a context pack with company facts, rules, good/bad examples, data dictionary, voice/quality standards and an update owner.
- Exit test: a fresh session or teammate can produce a competent result without a long oral briefing.

## 6. Build the smallest useful workflow

**Objective:** Start with one trigger, one bounded transformation or decision and one valuable output; retain human review while learning.

- Primary: [Claude Cowork for Agencies](https://maven.com/happy-operators/claude-cowork-for-agencies)
- Secondary: [Build an AI Agent with n8n & Slack](https://www.lewagon.com/events/build-an-ai-agent-with-n8n-slack-2bOY5X)
- Artifact: a manual-trigger prototype using real inputs, with every tool, transformation, decision and checkpoint documented.
- Exit test: it completes a real case end-to-end and is simpler than the original design.

## 7. Connect data and tools safely

**Objective:** Understand APIs, webhooks, connectors, data movement, credentials, privacy, retention and least privilege.

- Primary: [Grow Your Leads with API Automations](https://www.lewagon.com/events/grow-your-leads-with-api-automations-J2JaEB)
- Secondary: [What is Hapi?](https://speakhapi.com/blog/what-is-hapi)
- Artifact: a data-flow diagram, field classification, credential/permission plan and manual fallback.
- Exit test: the workflow exposes only the minimum data and permissions necessary, with a known revocation path.

## 8. Evaluate reliability on real cases

**Objective:** Create representative and edge-case tests; define expected outcomes, pass thresholds, review rules, cost and latency limits.

- Primary: [Build Resilience into Your Workflows](https://maven.com/p/0a68cf/build-resilience-into-your-workflows-with-claude-code)
- Secondary: [Why AI Automations Fail](https://maven.com/p/2b7e26/why-ai-automations-fail-top-mistakes-how-to-fix-them)
- Artifact: at least 20 cases, a failure taxonomy, an independent validator/challenger and regression reruns after changes.
- Exit test: the workflow meets written quality, exception, cost and human-intervention thresholds—not merely a convincing demo.

## 9. Launch, hand over and recover

**Objective:** Install ownership, runbook, alerts, fallback, escalation, secrets handling, incident review and maintenance cadence.

- Primary: [Agentic Collapse](https://sysdom.ai/articles/agentic-collapse)
- Secondary: [Claude at Work](https://maven.com/happy-operators/become-happy-operator)
- Artifact: shadow/parallel run, one-page runbook and simulated broken-integration and bad-output incidents.
- Exit test: the internal owner can operate, stop, recover and explain the workflow without the original builder.

## 10. Measure realized value and scale

**Objective:** Compare actual value, quality, cost, adoption and burden with baseline; improve the workflow and prioritize the next portfolio.

- Primary: [Scaling AI](https://online.hbs.edu/blog/post/scaling-ai)
- Secondary: [Nucleations: ARIA and IMPACT](https://nucleations.ai/)
- Artifact: a 30-day scorecard covering volume, time/case, quality, exceptions, cost/case, adoption, burden and incidents, plus the next three workflows.
- Exit test: the company can show realized—not forecast—value and has evidence for continuing, changing or stopping.

# What Alexey can own that the alternatives do not fully own

It would be inaccurate to say that competitors never mention testing, security, monitoring, ROI or ownership. They do. The defensible opportunity is to make the entire standard **mandatory, measurable and supported through launch**.

## 1. Published production-acceptance contract

Require a checklist or service-level target for quality, latency, cost per case, case coverage, exception rate and permitted human intervention.

> The workflow does not graduate when it demos. It graduates when it passes a published production-readiness review.

Publish anonymized scorecards, pass/rework rates and the actual acceptance rubric.

## 2. Shadow mode and staged go-live

Run the new workflow alongside the old process for an agreed case volume, reconcile outputs and expand authority only after evidence supports it.

> We support the workflow through shadow mode, controlled go-live and its first operating month.

## 3. Realized-value reconciliation

Create a workflow P&L comparing baseline with actual capacity, errors, revenue effect, software and maintenance costs.

> You leave with measured economic evidence—not an automation ROI estimate.

Publish 30/60/90-day forecast-versus-actual case studies.

## 4. Incident response and continuity

Every workflow ships with a kill switch, manual fallback, alerts, escalation, rollback and postmortem template.

> The process remains operable when the model, connector or data fails.

Demonstrate failure drills and publish anonymized postmortems.

## 5. Regression testing and change control

Version prompts, context and configuration; maintain a regression set; require release approval; define model/vendor-change and rollback procedures.

> Every change is testable, reversible and owned.

## 6. Security threat model as a deliverable

Require data classification, least privilege, secret storage, retention, prompt-injection controls and audit trails.

> Your workflow ships with explicit data boundaries and revocable authority.

## 7. Owner-independence test

A trained internal owner must run, diagnose and recover the workflow without Alexey or the original builder.

> The system is company-owned, not consultant-dependent.

Track handover-test completion and the percentage of workflows operating under owner control after 30 days.

## 8. Unified observability cockpit

Combine business value, quality, cost, failures, overrides, adoption and improvement backlog in one dashboard.

> See whether the workflow is working for the business—not merely whether it ran.

## 9. Maintenance calendar, RACI and total cost of ownership

Define the service owner, monthly and quarterly checks, vendor costs, data updates, training needs and portability plan.

> Know who maintains the asset, what it costs and how it survives vendor change.

## 10. Company deployment pod

Sell one company engagement for an executive sponsor, process owner and internal operator/implementer rather than one learner seat.

> One company, one flagship workflow, one accountable deployment pod.

## 11. Feasibility gate and bounded outcome promise

Before implementation, require accessible inputs, an accountable owner, enough workflow volume, authority to change the process and a credible value case.

> We accept only workflows with accessible inputs, a named owner and a credible value case.

Report acceptance, redirection and deployment rates.

## 12. Evidence-rich case library from Alexey's own operations

For each workflow publish before/after metrics, architecture, approval boundaries, failures, cost and current owner.

> See the exact systems, controls and economics behind an AI-native operating business.

## 13. Flagship workflow plus shared primitives for the next ten

Deliver the first operating workflow together with reusable context/data components, standards, templates and a prioritized portfolio.

> Deploy one flagship workflow. Install the operating system for the next ten.

Measure how quickly companies can deploy the second workflow without external help.
