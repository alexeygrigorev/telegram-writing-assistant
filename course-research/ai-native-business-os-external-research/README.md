## Coverage

- **53 annotated resources** across articles, videos, podcasts, tutorials, implementation guides, and short-form technical material.
- **22 implementation cases** with the operational problem, system role, human-control boundary, reported outcome, teaching lesson, and evidence quality.
- **5 lesson phases:** Map, Specify, Build, Prove, and Run.

## How to use the pack

Start with the guide's recommended 12 resources. Use the phase files when preparing individual lessons, the case-study files for discussion and reverse-engineering exercises, and the lesson map to connect readings to classroom deliverables.

Reported outcomes are preserved from their sources. Many figures are customer-, vendor-, or practitioner-reported rather than independently audited; the evidence labels and notes make that distinction explicit.
