## R01 — How AI-native companies turn workflows into operating capability

- **Phase:** Cross-phase
- **Priority:** Core
- **Format:** Article + case pack
- **Publisher / speaker:** OpenAI
- **Published / updated:** 2026-09-01
- **Evidence type:** Primary company article; customer-reported details

**Why it is relevant.** Basis, Clay, and Exa show how a recurring workflow becomes a reusable operating capability through triggers, context, tools, evidence, review points, ownership, and metrics.

**Best classroom use.** Use as the course-opening reading and ask learners to identify each element of Map → Specify → Build → Prove → Run in the three examples.

**Source:** [https://openai.com/index/ai-native-company-workflows/](https://openai.com/index/ai-native-company-workflows/)

## R02 — Reinventing the Operating System of Work with AI

- **Phase:** Cross-phase / Scale
- **Priority:** Core
- **Format:** Strategy article
- **Publisher / speaker:** BCG
- **Published / updated:** 2026-06
- **Evidence type:** Consulting analysis; client-reported case metrics

**Why it is relevant.** Makes the strategic case that task-level copilots are insufficient; value comes from redesigning end-to-end processes, controls, ownership, and governance.

**Best classroom use.** Use to distinguish AI productivity from an AI-native operating model. Debate the claim that the unit of change should be the whole process.

**Source:** [https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/reinventing-the-operating-system-of-work-with-ai](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/reinventing-the-operating-system-of-work-with-ai)

## R05 — Real-World Process Map Examples (+ Expert Tips)

- **Phase:** Map
- **Priority:** Core
- **Format:** Practical article
- **Publisher / speaker:** Miro
- **Published / updated:** 2026-02-10

## R13 — Every's Head of Consulting Just Automated Her Job

- **Phase:** Map / Specify
- **Priority:** Core
- **Format:** Podcast + transcript
- **Publisher / speaker:** Every / Natalia Quintero
- **Published / updated:** 2026-02-04
- **Evidence type:** Practitioner media; self-reported results

**Why it is relevant.** Shows granular task mapping, proprietary context, an AI project manager with a detailed job description, repeated rebuilds, and a claimed reduction from 15 project-management hours per week to one.

**Best classroom use.** Use as a nontechnical operator case. Ask learners to reconstruct Claudie's job description and identify the missing acceptance tests.
